How attention is measured

Coverage share is equally weighted by publisher group across comparable healthy sources. Counts represent distinct events, with one observation per event, publisher and week. Up and Down require a material change in share and multiple events. New means first observed after 28 days of verified history in this corpus. These are news attention signals; they do not measure market adoption or sentiment.
