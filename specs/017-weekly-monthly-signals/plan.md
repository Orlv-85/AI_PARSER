# Implementation Plan: Weekly and Monthly AI Signals

**Branch**: `017-weekly-monthly-signals` | **Date**: 2026-10-28 | **Spec**: [spec.md](spec.md)
**Input**: `spec.md`, `src/app/core/daily/signals.md` (current behavior of 006/007), facts from prod
(read-only, 2026-10-28)

## Summary

Add weekly (Monday-Sunday UTC) and monthly (calendar month UTC) views that are sums and comparisons
of the 006 data: daily snapshots, evidence and entities. Numbers are deterministic ($0). An optional
short brief per period is the only paid step (about $0.01 per week, $0.02 per month), off by default.
Retire weekly v2: delete its code, settings, cron, tests and the two collection hooks that still write
to `weekly_*` tables on every fetch; drop its tables in a later, separate release.

**Language/Version**: Python 3.14 | **Web**: FastAPI, server-rendered page + small JS (like `/`)
**Storage**: SQLAlchemy + Alembic; SQLite dev, PostgreSQL prod; two new additive tables

**Scale**: about 60-90 digest items a weekday, 5-15 at weekends; a week is 250-450 articles and about
1,700-2,700 mentions; computation is in-memory over those rows

and the previous two weeks, "confirmed" labels (New, Up, Down) only under strict gates, and a

## Design of the new feature

Main module: `src/app/core/periodic/signals_period.py`; code doc
`src/app/core/periodic/signals_period.md` (registered in `docs/README.md`, `README.md`,
`src/app/README.md`; header `module: src/app/core/periodic/signals_period.py`, covers
`src/app/core/periodic/*.py`, `src/app/storage/period_signals*.py`,
`src/app/storage/migrations/versions/*period_signal*.py`, `src/app/web/period_view.py`,
`src/app/web/routes/period.py`). Name decided 2026-10-28 (planner): a package of its own, not
`core/daily/`, because it reads daily snapshots and is not daily; prefix `period_` keeps tables and
CLI distinct from `weekly_*`.

### Definitions (decided 2026-10-28 (planner))

- Week: Monday 00:00 to Sunday 23:59 UTC of digest days D (the unit of 006). Report key: Monday date.
  Month: calendar month UTC. Key: first day. Matches the Monday-Sunday weeks the old Monday hook used.
- Only completed periods. A week is computable from the Monday after (02:30 UTC, after the daily
  signals run of the Sunday digest at 02:00 UTC); a month from the 1st.
- First period: the week of 2026-10-12 (first full week with snapshots from `COMPARABLE_FROM`
  2026-10-10); earlier days are history only. Comparison needs two ready periods: first comparison is
  the week of 10-19; first monthly view is November 2026 (ready 2026-12-01), compared with October
  only if October is `ready` (it will be partial: data from 10-12 only, so no October comparison
  in 2026; first monthly comparison in January 2027).

### Computation (all $0, pure functions over stored data)

Inputs per period (`PeriodInput`): the daily snapshots of its days (`daily_signal_reports`), per-day
coverage (`coverage` column: items, extracted), mentions with entity keys (`signal_mentions`,
`ai_entities`) for counts and links, `daily_signal_scope` for publishers, `daily_signal_briefs` only
for the brief step. Event types come from the snapshots (chips), not from `article_signals`
(rows disappear with article retention; snapshots do not).

| Section | How | v1 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coverage line | days complete / days in period; items; extracted share; distinct publishers; quiet flag | yes |
| Top stories | union of daily `top_stories` of the period; same story across days merged by event type + entity key; ranked by distinct publishers, then days; at most `week_top_max`, `month_top_max` | yes |
| Top entities | by type group (models, products, companies, technologies): ranked by distinct articles then publishers, with `top_entity_min_articles` and `top_entity_min_publishers`; at most `top_entity_max` per group; each with days present and links | yes |
| New this period | union of daily `new` lists in the period, confirmed first, `new_period_max`; shows first-seen day | yes |
| Risers and coolers (week) | exactly the computation behind daily Rising/Cooling with D = the Sunday: last 7 days against the previous 7; reuse the Sunday snapshot sections if its methodology is current, else call the shared helper | yes |
| Risers and coolers (month) | the same helper extracted from `signals_compute.py` (`trend_delta`) applied to month totals against the previous month, with its own minimums | monthly phase |
| Event-type mix | sum of daily event chips (stories per `HARD_EVENTS` type, rest as "+N more") with delta against the previous period | yes |
| Publisher breadth | distinct publishers of the period against the previous period; below `breadth_ratio` the comparison is suppressed with reason `low_publisher_coverage` | yes |
| Brief | one paid call, see Cost | phase after v1 numbers |
| Day-by-day volume strip | items per day of the period | later |
| Per-vendor view, entity pages, CSV export | | later |

Weekly Rising/Cooling must not drift from the daily one: the extraction of `trend_delta` from
`signals_compute.py` is covered by the existing daily tests and must leave the daily snapshot
payload hashes unchanged (no `METHODOLOGY_VERSION` bump; check on the fixtures of 006).
Open for the implementer to verify in the code: that the daily Rising window ending on D includes D
(then the Sunday snapshot is exactly the week). If it ends on D-1, use the helper with the week bounds.

### Status and coverage rules

Constants in `PeriodThresholds` (recorded in every stored payload; changing them is a release with a
`PERIOD_METHODOLOGY_VERSION` bump and a $0 `--recompute-all`):

| Constant | Week | Month | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `min_complete_days` | 6 of 7 | 24 of 28-31 | days whose digest is published and extracted at `complete_ratio` (006 rule). A missing day is not zero |
| `min_items` | 100 | 400 | items in scope over the period |
| `breadth_ratio` | 0.7 | 0.7 | publishers of this period / previous period; below it, no comparison |
| `quiet_items` | 150 | 600 | below: sections still shown, opening line says "quiet week" |

Status: `unavailable` (no snapshots), `partial` (fewer complete days or items than minimum),
`building_history` (ready but previous period not ready: no comparison), `ready`.
Coverage is therefore judged on what the reader sees (digest days) and on publisher breadth as the
proxy for source outages; per-source fetch-slot health is not kept (closes F1). Prod check: all
weeks since 10-12 qualify (397 items in 10-12 week; weekend days are small but complete).

### Storage (additive migration, `down_revision` = current head)

| Table | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `period_signal_reports` | one row per (`period_kind`, `period_start`): `period_end`, `status`, `methodology_version`, `extraction_version`, `payload_hash`, `thresholds`, `payload` JSON, `coverage` JSON, timestamps; unique on kind+start |
| `period_signal_briefs` | one row per (`period_kind`, `period_start`): `status` `ok`/`invalid`, `input_hash`, `model`, `payload`, timestamps |

No FK to `articles` (evidence and snapshots survive retention); links in the payload are article
ids and URLs captured at compute time, same as 006. Recompute writes a row only when `payload_hash`
changes. Migration skips objects `create_all()` made (as in 006/007).

### Versions (as in 006)

| Constant | Bump when | After deploy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `PERIOD_METHODOLOGY_VERSION` | thresholds or section rules change | `ai-news period-signals --recompute-all` ($0) |
| `PERIOD_BRIEF_VERSION` | brief prompt, packet or validation change | brief backfill (paid, capped) |
| daily `METHODOLOGY_VERSION` / `EXTRACTION_VERSION` | owned by 006 | a daily recompute changes snapshots; the next period run picks it up through the payload hash |

### Run and backfill

- CLI `ai-news period-signals [--kind week|month] [--period YYYY-MM-DD] [--backfill N]
  [--recompute-all] [--with-brief] [--max-usd X] [--max-seconds N]`. Default: the latest completed
  week, and the latest completed month on the 1st. Idempotent; safe to run every night.
- Job: one more step at the end of `scripts/run_daily_digest_job.sh`, after daily signals, same
  guard style (time left in the budget, failure never changes the digest result). It runs nightly and
  does work only when a completed period is missing or its inputs changed (cheap hash compare).
- Backfill of past weeks: `--backfill 3` computes 10-12, 10-19, 10-26 at $0 right after deploy;
  weeks before 10-12 are never computed (history only). Brief backfill is a separate paid flag.

### Web

- `GET /weekly` page (latest ready week; `?week=YYYY-MM-DD` Monday), `GET /monthly` (monthly phase),
  both public. API `GET /api/news/period-signals?kind=week&start=YYYY-MM-DD` (stored data only;
  `brief` null when missing; unavailable state, never 500). `GET /api/news/period-signals/index`
  lists available periods for the picker.
- Relation to `/`: the daily block is unchanged and stays the home. Under it, a one-line link
  "Week of Oct 19-25: weekly signals" replaces the legacy preview (stage 3). The header nav gets
  "Weekly" (and "Monthly" when a month exists). Weekly page items link to `/?date=D` for the days.
- Rendering: server HTML in `web/period_view.py`; shared classes with the daily block; a client
  re-render is not needed (no date switching in v1; the picker is plain links). Page script and
  styles go to `web/static/` (no inline, like `/`).

### Brief (paid, phase after v1 numbers)

Same method as 007: packet from the stored period payload plus digest titles and summaries of the
top stories (cap `PERIOD_PACKET_MAX_BYTES` 16,000 for a week, 24,000 for a month), one DeepSeek
call, mechanical validation by reusing `parse_brief` with parameterised sentence counts (week 4-6,
month 5-8), one recovery attempt, then `invalid` and no brief. Same `input_hash` is never paid twice.
Model `deepseek-v4-pro` through `BRIEF_PRICES` (unknown model: no call). Run through the 006
`SignalsLedger` with its own run id so caps and replay work unchanged.

Flags (defaults off; deploy workflows do not set them, as for 006):

| Flag | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| `AINEWS_PERIOD_SIGNALS_ENABLED` | the nightly step and the CLI compute and store periods; pages/API show them when rows exist (no row: unavailable state, nav link hidden) |

## Risks

- Sunday snapshot not ready (daily signals failed): the weekly run falls back to the helper; if the
  days are incomplete the week is `partial`, not wrong.
- Operator entity merges after a week is stored change risers: covered by recompute; the page shows
  the compute time.
- Weekend-heavy or holiday weeks look "quiet": explicit quiet line, not a hidden low-quality chart.
- The `trend_delta` extraction touches 006 code: guarded by unchanged payload hashes on fixtures.
- Dropping v2 tables is irreversible without the dump: separate release, restore tested first.
